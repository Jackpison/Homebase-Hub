Your own start page, bookmarks, notes and tasks, on your own server.

Homebase Hub is a self-hosted personal workspace: your start page, links, notes, cards, tasks and browser tools. One login, no database, and your data stays on your own hosting.

InstallRead the GuideView on GitHub

One place

Home, Links, Info, Cards, Tasks and Tools share one login, one Settings page, one backup and one look.

Private by design

Optional encrypted tabs, two-step login, login history and devices, and data files that cannot be opened from a browser.

Runs on ordinary hosting

PHP 8.1 or newer and a place to put files. No database and no build step on your server.

Updates you control

Update from GitHub with one click and your password, or upload a zip. Every update is checked first and can be undone.
