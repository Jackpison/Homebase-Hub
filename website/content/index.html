In detail

What each page does

Each page does one job, and they all look like they belong together.

Home

Your start page, with background photos that change and their credit in the corner.

  • Photos get a phone-sized copy, so they load fast.
  • Show it with or without the bar.

Links

Bookmarks in tabs and categories.

  • Link icons are saved on your server.
  • Colours and text sizes for each tab.
  • Tabs can be encrypted with your passphrase.

Info

Notes in tabs. Each tab holds 1 to 12 boxes.

  • Arrange boxes as cards, or write the tab as one document with an outline.
  • Encrypted tabs for private notes.
  • Export a tab or all of them as PDF or text.

Cards

Your cards in a table you can search and filter by bank, network, type and holder.

  • Yearly charges added up.
  • Nothing is sent until you are logged in.

Tasks

Things with a due date: bills, renewals, chores.

  • Repeats, prices, categories and smart lists.
  • A calendar, analytics and a Year in Review PDF.
  • Reminders in Telegram.

Tools

Small tools that run in your browser: crop, resize and convert images, merge and sign PDFs, read text from images, remove backgrounds, make QR codes, and more.

  • Star favourites and arrange your own sections.
Telegram

Reminders and backups, sent to your phone

Security

Private by design

Your data lives in one folder on your own hosting, closed to browsers. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you set it up.

{{i:key}}

One login, done properly

Two-step login, recovery codes, trusted browsers, a device list, and logins paused after five wrong tries.

{{i:lock}}

Encrypted where you choose

Encrypted tabs in Links and Info, and encrypted backup files. The keys are made in your browser.

{{i:download}}

Backups you can restore

One file holds every page. Earlier versions are kept, and Telegram can send backups to your chat.

{{i:refresh}}

Updates you can undo

Update from GitHub or with a zip. Each update is checked first, needs your password, and can be undone.

Install

Get started in three steps

You need web hosting with PHP 8.3 or newer and a file manager. HTTPS is recommended.

  1. Download

    Get site-update-{{VER}}.zip from the latest release.

  2. Upload and extract

    Put the zip in your domain's folder in the file manager, and extract it there.

  3. Create your site

    Open your domain, click the key icon, and enter the setup code from site-data/core/SETUP-CODE.php with a new password.
