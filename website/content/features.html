Features

Six pages and one Settings page. Everything here is in the current release.

Home

Your start page, with background photos that rotate. Photos get a phone-sized copy so the page loads faster on a phone.

Links

Bookmarks in tabs and categories, saved link icons, text sizes and colours per tab, and optional encrypted tabs locked with your passphrase.

Info

Notes in tabs. Each tab holds from 1 to 12 boxes in sizes from one third to full width, laid out as Notebook Cards or as a Document. Tabs can be encrypted, and you can export a tab or all tabs as PDF or text.

Cards

Your cards in a table you can search. No card data is sent until you are logged in.

Tasks

Things with due dates: repeats, prices, categories, smart lists (Today, Overdue, This Week, This Month, Paused), a calendar, analytics and a Year in Review PDF. Reminders can come through Telegram.

Tools

Small tools that run in your browser: crop, resize, compress and convert images; merge, split, compress, sign and protect PDFs; text from images (OCR); background removal; QR codes; hashes; JSON formatting; zip files; and more.

Settings

Overview and Guided Setup

Where things stand and what to look at next, with a guided setup for the protection steps.

Appearance

166 presets, your own saved presets, colours for each mode, and a separate accent for each page.

Security

Password, two-step login, login approval in Telegram, trusted browsers, devices, and encryption.

Telegram

Reminders, backups to your chat, login alerts and instant replies, with the schedule explained step by step.

Backup and Restore

One file holds every page and its settings. Export data as CSV or JSON, restore earlier versions, or Start Over.

Updates and Health

Update from GitHub or a zip, go back in one click, find files that do not belong, and check disk, speed, errors and your domains.

On a phone the Settings menu comes first and each section opens with a back button.
